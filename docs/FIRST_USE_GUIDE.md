# First-use guide (draft)

## Company administrator
1. Install and open KingsFortProperty.
2. Choose **Create company account**.
3. Enter the company's name, official email and contact details.
4. Create the first administrator using a company-controlled email address and a strong unique password.
5. Complete company settings, timezone and logo after entering the workspace.
6. Invite staff by their individual work email addresses and assign only the role they need.
7. Review the staff list and confirm access before adding business records.
8. Register properties and submit them for approval where required.
9. Configure email/WhatsApp notification preferences and subscription billing when those integrations are enabled.

## Staff member
1. Open the invitation sent to your work email.
2. Confirm your name and set a strong password.
3. Sign in using your company workspace and email address.
4. Complete your profile and follow the role-specific tour.
5. Use **Skip guide** to go straight to the workspace; reopen it later from **Help & Instructions**.

## Roles
- **Company Administrator:** full control of company workspace, staff, roles and settings.
- **Manager:** operational oversight, property approvals and management reports.
- **HR:** staff directory and employee administration functions as configured by the company.
- **Accountant:** rent, payment and financial reporting functions.
- **Property Agent:** property records, leads, inspections and assigned customer workflows.
- **Viewer:** read-only access to permitted business information.

The final permission matrix is configurable and must be validated with the customer before production rollout. The role labels above are not a substitute for endpoint-level authorisation tests.
